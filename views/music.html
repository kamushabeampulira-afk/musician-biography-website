Career Timeline

2008

Beginning of Music Career

Eddy Kenzo began his professional music journey and released his first song, "Yannimba," with Mikie Wine.

2010

Stamina Breakthrough

His song "Stamina" became a major hit and helped establish him as one of Uganda's emerging music artists.

2014

Sitya Loss

"Sitya Loss" became an international success, with its music video featuring the Ghetto Kids gaining worldwide attention.

2015

BET Award & Foundation

Kenzo won a BET Award and founded the Eddy Kenzo Foundation, supporting underprivileged young people.

2018

Nickelodeon Award

He received the Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Award for Favorite African Star.

2019

Big Talent Soccer Academy

Kenzo opened the Big Talent Soccer Academy to help young people develop their sporting and educational potential.
