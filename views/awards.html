Awards & Achievements

Throughout his career, Eddy Kenzo has received recognition for his contribution to Ugandan and African music. From BET to a Grammy nomination, these are the milestones that mark his journey.

2015

BET Award

Won Viewer's Choice Best New International Artist — becoming the first East African artist to take home a BET Award.

2018

Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Award

Named Favorite African Star at the Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Awards.

Multiple Years

All Africa Music Awards

Repeated recognition at the All Africa Music Awards, including Best East African Male Artist.

2022

Grammy Nomination

First Ugandan musician nominated for a Grammy, for "Gimme Love" with Matt B.
