Awards & Achievements

Throughout his career, Eddy Kenzo has received recognition for his contribution to Ugandan and African music.

2015

BET Award

Eddy Kenzo won the BET Award for Viewer's Choice Best New International Artist.

2018

Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Award

He received the Favorite African Star award at the Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Awards.

Multiple Years

All Africa Music Awards

Kenzo has received recognition at the All Africa Music Awards, including recognition as a leading East African male artist.

2022

Grammy Nomination

Eddy Kenzo was involved in the Grammy-nominated song "Gimme Love" with Matt B.
