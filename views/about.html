About Eddy Kenzo

From the streets of Masaka to the global stage.

Eddy Kenzo is a Ugandan singer, songwriter, and dancer who rose from homelessness to become one of East Africa's most recognized musical voices. Blending dancehall, Afrobeat, and traditional Ugandan rhythms, he turned personal hardship into a message of hope that resonates worldwide.

The Story So Far

Born Edrisah Kenzo Musuuza on 25 December 1989 in Masaka, he lost his mother at four and spent over a decade living on the streets of Masaka and Kampala. A brief football scholarship gave him a foothold, but it was music that changed his life — first with "Yannimba" in 2008, then the breakout hit "Stamina" in 2010.

International fame arrived in 2014 with "Sitya Loss," whose video featuring the Ghetto Kids went viral and caught the attention of Sean Combs. Since then he has broken barriers for East African artists and used his platform to give back through the Eddy Kenzo Foundation.

Why He Matters

  • First East African BET winner — Viewer's Choice Best New International Artist (2015).
  • First Ugandan Grammy nominee — for "Gimme Love" with Matt B (2022).
  • Global viral moment — "Sitya Loss" and the Ghetto Kids introduced Ugandan music to millions.
  • Philanthropist — founder of the Eddy Kenzo Foundation (2015) and the Big Talent Soccer Academy (2019).
"I wanted to choose to be better, not bitter. I went through a lot, but it does not mean that someone else should go through the same."

— Eddy Kenzo
