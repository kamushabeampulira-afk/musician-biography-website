About Eddy Kenzo

Early Life & Education

Born Edrisah Kenzo Musuuza on 25th December 1989 in Masaka, Uganda [citation:1]. His mother passed away when he was only four years old [citation:5][citation:7]. He spent the next 13 years living on the streets of Masaka and Kampala [citation:1][citation:5].

Initially, Kenzo aspired to be a professional footballer and joined Masaka Local Council FC's camp at the age of nine. He received a sports bursary to attend Lubiri Secondary School in Kampala but didn't complete his studies [citation:1].

Musical Journey & Breakthrough

Kenzo began making music in 2008, releasing his first song "Yannimba" with Mikie Wine [citation:1]. His breakthrough came in 2010 with the single "Stamina," which became a massive hit and was used as a theme song during the 2011 Ugandan general election [citation:1][citation:5].

His international breakthrough arrived in 2014 with "Sitya Loss" and an accompanying viral video featuring the Ghetto Kids. The video caught the attention of record executive Sean Combs and went viral [citation:1].

Awards & Achievements

  • BET Award (2015) - Viewer's Choice Best New International Artist. He was the first East African artist to win a BET Award [citation:1][citation:4].
  • Nickelodeon Kids' Choice Award (2018) - Favorite African Star [citation:1].
  • Multiple All Africa Music Awards - Including Best East African Male Artist [citation:1].
  • Grammy Nomination (2022) - First Ugandan musician to receive a Grammy nomination for "Gimme Love" with Matt B [citation:5][citation:7].

Philanthropy & Legacy

Kenzo founded the Eddy Kenzo Foundation in 2015, a charitable organization supporting underprivileged youth [citation:1][citation:7]. He also opened the Big Talent Soccer Academy in Kampala in 2019, which identifies local youth talent and provides education to help foster their skills [citation:1].

"I wanted to choose to be better, not bitter. I went through a lot, but it does not mean that someone else should go through the same." - Eddy Kenzo [citation:5]

His dream is to create a sustainable, scalable platform for ghetto children to change their own lives [citation:9].
